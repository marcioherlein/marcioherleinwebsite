2026-11-05 · Production build `http://127.0.0.1:4177` · Desktop 1440 × 1000; phone 390 × 844.

This is a **simulated assessment**, not real customer research, approval, or evidence of conversion. I used the same operations/finance/risk project-buyer perspective as the [earlier review](2026-11-production-client-review.md). This was a targeted rendered-browser follow-up: homepage and all three case pages on both viewports, the guided cost lesson, one identity example, secondary image inspection, and the cold-load contact path. No application edits, source inspection, external communications, or commits were made.

**The redesign resolves the main explanation and inquiry problems.** I can now identify work relevant to my team, understand what information goes in and what comes out, see Marcio's personal contribution, and choose a project inquiry without confusing it with recruitment. The remaining substantive uncertainty concerns completed output evidence and operational readiness, rather than a need for more persuasive wording.

**The three projects are now distinguishable.** ERM is a platform Marcio says he built from scratch at SAP and that risk managers use; its inputs, connected portfolio/assessment output, and application design/development contribution are explicit. Insic identifies people researching listed companies, financial/model inputs, scenarios and saved context, and his application/calculation/normalization/persistence/automation work; **independent prototype** is plainly stated. GlobalShipping identifies Argentine import buyers, supplier/product/commercial inputs, a comparison and dated saved quote, and his application/classification/cost work. Its page specifically limits the demonstrated evidence to local frontend views and modeled calculations; live providers, accounts, deployment, and adoption remain unverified. This is a clearer boundary, not proof of a live end-to-end service.

**The revised cost example teaches a specific lesson.** I followed “25 standard, then 25 compact” and selected Compare costs on desktop and phone. At the same 25 units and 12.5 kg of goods, the lowest modeled total changes from **Air: $1,194.68** to **Courier: $883.85**, a **$310.83** difference. Volume changes from 0.20 to 0.05 m³; the page explains chargeable weight and sea minimums. The result is interpreted rather than left as an unexplained number. Invented freight rates and illustrative tax assumptions remain visible. This demonstrates a useful calculation interface and an input-sensitive decision comparison; it establishes neither real freight savings nor customs accuracy. The three-description identity helper remains correctly bounded and returned Battery / “lithium battery” for the replacement-battery example.

**The editorial artifact improves concreteness without proving automation.** The visible questions in the Insic case show the shape of editorial review. “Held · not published,” the date, and the explicit statement that this is not evidence of an automated or published post prevent an unsupported inference. It remains a short editorial excerpt presented as a saved draft, rather than an inspected sourced briefing, machine-generated draft, approval record, or delivery trace. Its provenance was not independently checked in this browser review.

**Material gaps that remain require evidence.**

- **ERM:** no complete authorized assessment/report or portfolio-to-review example is publicly inspectable. The new workflow is understandable, but the quality of its final business artifact still cannot be assessed. Internal confidentiality may reasonably limit what can be shown.
- **Insic:** no actual changed-assumption → saved-reference record, sourced HTML briefing, or automation review/delivery record is shown. The held excerpt supports editorial thinking, not reliable generation or publishing. Historical interface views remain historical.
- **GlobalShipping:** no full catalog-candidate/clarification/review result or actual dated saved quote is inspectable. The working cost lesson does not demonstrate account persistence, live integrations, or complete classification. Deployment, adoption, and support readiness require factual confirmation if relevant to a commission.
- **Commissioning:** availability, ownership, acceptance checks, deployment access, and support terms still need discussion. The redesigned inquiry is an appropriate place to resolve them. The website should not invent these commitments or claim outcomes to close the evidence gaps.

One minor presentation issue remains in the captured comparison table: mode, “Lowest,” and billed basis run together, such as `AirLowest33.33 kg chargeable`. Separating those labels would improve scanning. It did not prevent understanding the totals or lesson and is distinct from the evidence gaps above.

As this simulated buyer, I now know why and how to start a project discussion. I would still need a scoped walkthrough and suitable output evidence before commissioning implementation. That is a qualitative assessment of the page, not a prediction of inquiries or sales.
